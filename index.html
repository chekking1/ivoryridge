The part most mailers leave out
The sale price isn't always what the seller walked away with.

In this market, a lot of sellers help buyers with closing costs or a rate buydown, and those credits don't always show up in the public numbers. When I run the numbers for your home, I'll account for them, so you see a real net and not just a headline price.
